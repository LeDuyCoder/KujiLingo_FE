# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese speakers who study Japanese independently. They use KujiLingo to follow a learning path and return to lessons and reviews across web and mobile.

## Product Purpose

KujiLingo helps independent learners build Japanese skills through structured courses, lessons, vocabulary and kanji practice, and review. It makes the next useful study action clear and helps learners make steady progress.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, motivating, and clear. Encourage learners with useful feedback and visible progress while keeping the tone respectful and easy to understand. No external brand references were specified.

## Anti-references

- Do not make learners feel pressured or guilty about missed study days or streaks.
- Do not let gamification compete with or distract from learning.
- Avoid clutter that obscures the next useful learning action.

## Design Principles

- Put the learning task first; use rewards and progress indicators to support it.
- Make the next lesson or review action easy to find.
- Show progress clearly and encourage steady practice without guilt.
- Keep explanations and feedback friendly, concise, and specific.
- Use consistent, accessible interaction patterns across web and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support reduced motion, maintain readable contrast, provide keyboard-accessible interactions on web, and do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
